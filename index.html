          QUESTION BANK

          Questions everyone should prepare

          01

          Tell me about yourself.

          Build a concise narrative around who you are, what shaped you, and why medicine.

          02

          Why medicine?

          Show the evolution of your motivation through specific experiences.

          03

          Why this school?

          Know the school well enough to explain fit specifically.

          04

          What are your strengths?

          Never stop at adjectives. Prove the strength with a story.

          05

          What are your weaknesses?

          Choose something genuine, explain your response, and show growth.

          06

          Why should we choose you?

          Connect your experiences and contributions without sounding entitled.

          07

          Where do you see yourself in 10 years?

          Show direction while leaving room for growth and uncertainty.

          Questions to ask your interviewer

          School-focused

          • What do you believe is the greatest strength of this medical school?
          • What is your favorite aspect of the school?
          • What distinguishes students here from students at other schools?

          Interviewer-focused

          • What led you to choose your specialty?
          • Was there an experience that influenced your career path?
          • Looking back, is there anything you wish you had done differently?

          Big-picture medicine

          • What do you see as the biggest challenge facing healthcare?
          • How would you advise my generation of physicians to approach that challenge?

          Question-quality test

          Prefer questions that invite personal perspective over facts easily found on the school's website. You do not have to make every question exclusively about the school.

          Source-informed interview principles

          • Do not assume the interviewer has read every part of your application.
          • MMI stations evaluate your reasoning and communication—not a magic “correct” answer.
          • In group interviews, do not be the alpha or the sheep: contribute without dominating.
          • Build on other people's ideas and disagree respectfully.
          • Stay composed when challenged or criticized. Do not take the bait.
          • Be kind and professional to everyone you encounter on interview day.
          • Interview day is also your chance to evaluate whether the school is right for you.
